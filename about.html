About Saath

The legal system, made human

“Saath” means together — a companion by your side. That's exactly what we set out to build.

When most people in India face a legal situation — a notice, an FIR, a property dispute, a cheque bounce, a family emergency — they're hit with the same three feelings: confusion, fear, and a sense that the whole system is built for someone else. Lawyers feel expensive and intimidating. Documents are impossible to read. And the most basic question goes unanswered: “Where do I stand, and what do I do next?”

Saath exists to answer that question — calmly, clearly, and in plain language. It sits in your pocket and walks with you through your legal journey, one understandable step at a time.

What we believe

Understanding your situation shouldn't require a law degree. Everyone deserves to know their rights, understand the documents in their hands, and walk into a police station or courtroom prepared instead of afraid. Clarity is not a luxury — it's the first step toward justice.

Our approach

We keep three promises. Plain language — no jargon, ever. Honesty — Saath offers educational guidance, not legal advice, and always tells you when to consult a qualified advocate. And respect for your privacy — much of your information stays on your device, and we never sell your data.

An honest note

Saath is an independent, privately built app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or representing any government, court, or police authority, and it does not provide government services. Where we reference legal or government information, we point you to official sources such as India Code, NALSA, and eCourts. For advice about your specific situation, always consult a licensed advocate.

Made for India

Saath is designed around real Indian legal journeys — the notices, the hearings, the systems people actually deal with. It's built with care, in India, for anyone who has ever felt lost in front of the law.
